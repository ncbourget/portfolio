I began Garment Printing Club during my freshman year at MassArt to expand access to silkscreen facilities that were previously limited to Printmaking majors.

As a Communication Design major, I led weekly workshops for students across disciplines, teaching the basics of screenprinting, prepress, and garment production.

GPC became the most attended student organization on campus and earned Student Organization of the Year in 2024. The club supported first-time printers, experienced members, and three large exhibitions.

We also printed with student organizations across MassArt, including a Student Government shirt run for the 2024 Massachusetts Statehouse Visit after part of the MassArt tower building collapsed.

Before graduating, I delegated the club structure to a small team of experienced students so it could continue without me.
