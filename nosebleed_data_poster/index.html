Nosebleed Poster

Poster design

Spring 2023

01 Poster

Nosebleed Poster is an early poster design project from my first year in the program. It became one of the first pieces where the work started to feel resolved beyond the assignment itself.

My professor described it as portfolio-ready at the time, which made it a useful marker for the direction my design practice was beginning to take: direct, graphic, and built around a strong central image.
