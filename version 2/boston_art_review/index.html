Boston Art Review

Publication • Prepress

Summer 2025

01 RADAR issue #1 02 Public outreach 03 ñ press collaboration classifieds

I worked directly with Boston Art Review founder Jameson Johnson to develop the inaugural edition of RADAR, a community brochure publication. I led key visual decisions including long-term color strategy and Pantone spot color selections to help establish a lasting system for future editions.

I also refined and finalized the core map graphic used across the publication. Developed in part with Gabriel Sosa’s ñ press initiative, the project included public outreach graphics and an original classifieds sheet that was risographed and packed into Boston Art Review issue 15 in Fall 2025.
