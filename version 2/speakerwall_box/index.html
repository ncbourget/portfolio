Speakerwall Box

Electronics • Programming • Fabrication

Fall 2025

01 Box detail 02 Oiled book 03 Audioplayer detail 04 Selected spreads

Speakerwall Box is a music container built to house a book and a Raspberry Pi audio player. The project was made for Death Grips, focusing on the occult charge of a blank snakeskin leather case and the violent, handmade feeling of the object inside.

The interior was designed to resemble a homemade bomb, connecting the physical object to the band’s lyrical content and sound. The Raspberry Pi ran a custom Python program that played embedded audio files, with a button that boosted the sound by two decibels.

The companion book echoed the box’s material language. Each page was saturated with mineral oil until it became translucent, and the writing was divided into Skin, Muscle, and Bone, sections tied to the atmosphere of Death Grips’ three most important albums.
