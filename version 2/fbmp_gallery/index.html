FaceBook Marketplace Gallery

Web • 3D • Animation • Game

Winter 2026

01 Digital experience 02 Gallery space 03 Advertisements 04 Website

FaceBook Marketplace Gallery is an ongoing project started by a friend of mine, Arthur Michel, which turns the online space of FaceBook Marketplace into something ironically artistic and cerebral. I was on board with a lot of my friends for the first edition of the gallery show on January 4, 2026. I was in charge of making many advertisements, including letter-sized posters for wheatpasting, half-sheet flyers, and a minute-and-a-half 3D animation.

Once actually in the space at Box of Moonlight in Brooklyn, roughly 12 of us showed off our different art, mainly oil paintings. I had a spot right next to the door where I showed a digital experience I had been crafting for the last two months. The program was made in Godot and was a pseudo recreation of the gallery space we were standing in. There were many ragdolls that the user could throw around and blank picture frames on the walls. Once a photo was AirDropped to my MacBook, it would change all of the blank frames to that photo using an intense script for Godot and Apple Automator.

It was a hit, unexpectedly becoming a one of a kind photobooth that would leave a memory like no other.
