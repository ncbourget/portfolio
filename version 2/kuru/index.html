KURU Fine Dining

Branding • Craft

Spring 2026

01 Brand guidelines 02 Menus

KURU Fine Dining is a restaurant identity system for a fictional private dining institution built around excess, luxury, and consumption.

The visual language is warm, controlled, and cult-like, using refinement as a surface that makes the project feel intimate, decadent, and quietly uncomfortable.

Menus use perforation, receipt-like slips, and butcher-cut references to connect fine dining with disassembly. A mirror-polished steel menu board reflects the viewer back into the system.
