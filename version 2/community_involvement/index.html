Community Involvement

Silkscreen • Prepress • Instruction

Fall 2022—present

01 The Hardy Bunch Foundation 02 Mass. Statehouse Visit shirts 03 Planet Titan CMYK run 04 Carcass Publicity

My community involvement projects are chosen case by case, depending on how closely they connect to my message, my skills, and the people the work is meant to support.

From silkscreening shirts for nonprofits in Africa to designing posters for community art sales, I have always believed design has a place inside human community. These projects gather moments where print, prepress, and instruction became useful beyond the studio.
