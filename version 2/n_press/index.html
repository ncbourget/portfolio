ñ press

Leadership • Team-building • Publication • Graphics

April 2025—present

01 ñ Open Studio Days 02 ICA Watershed 03 Silkscreen Night 04 Graphic Work 05 Multiple Formats 06 Wheatpasting 07 Fiesta Fútbol Chelsea

ñ press is a community print studio founded during the first Boston Public Art Triennial. I was selected by Triennial artist Gabriel Sosa to lead silkscreen, instruction, design, and prepress operations for the project.

I directed technical production for large-scale public workshops, balancing community engagement with precise fabrication standards and adaptive problem-solving. I also worked with Gabriel to design and produce promotional posters and original silkscreen graphics for ñ press-specific and Triennial-related programming.

ñ press has continued beyond the Triennial as an ongoing public practice. At ñ open studio days, we teach people how to use fringe printmaking techniques, make direct political graphics, and build confidence with tools that can move between art, publishing, and activism.
