Challngr

UI/UX • Motion • Branding

Spring 2026

01 Motion graphic 02 Screens

Challngr is a Figma product design project that frames motivation through social pressure instead of individual tracking.

Users create short-term challenges, track live progress, and receive notifications when they are overtaken. A pegboard archive lets them collect images, videos, and medals tied to completed challenges.

The interface prioritizes direct action, real-time feedback, and clear outcomes, treating product design as a way to shape behavior rather than simply organize features.
